War Machine

92% Match 2017 TV-MA 2h 2m HD
Comedy Drama War

A satirical war story about a general who, with the help of a tight-knit group of loyal soldiers, seized control of NATO's war strategy in the Middle East, only to be taken down by a journalist's damning exposé. Based on the book "The Operators" by the late Michael Hastings and inspired by real events, this dark comedy stars Brad Pitt as a charismatic four-star general.

Cast: Brad Pitt, Anthony Michael Hall, Topher Grace, Will Poulter
Director: David Michôd
